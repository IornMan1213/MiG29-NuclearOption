MiG-29 Fulcrum

A flyable MiG-29 (9.12) for Nuclear Option, with its own flight model tuned to real-world data, R-27R, R-27T, R-73 and R-60M missiles built from scratch, the GSh-30-1 cannon, working canopy and gear doors, and four liveries.

Download latest release Source on GitHub

Flight model

Tuned offline with a Python re-implementation of Nuclear Option's per-part aerodynamics and engine thrust, against published MiG-29 figures.

  • All-moving stabilators for pitch and differential roll, plus rudders, ailerons and flaps. Every surface moves on the model.
  • RD-33 engines with an altitude thrust curve and afterburner. No thrust vectoring, as on the real 9.12.
  • 3.7 t internal fuel and an 11.5 t empty weight. Lift points are placed for a stable static margin.
  • Breakable airframe: hitboxes come from the base game's part colliders scaled to the MiG, so parts break off and scorch realistically.

Weapons

Four missiles and their launch rails, modelled from scratch to published dimensions, plus the MiG's own cannon. Each wing has three pylons, matching the real aircraft.

  • Inner pylon: R-27R or R-27T on an AKU-470 rail, R-73, R-60M, or stock stores
  • Middle pylon: R-73 on an APU-73 rail, R-60M on an APU-60 rail, or stock stores
  • Outer pylon: R-73 or R-60M
  • Presets: air superiority, dogfight and strike loadouts, which AI-flown MiGs also use

Airframe & cockpit

The details that make it feel like a MiG rather than a reskin.

  • MiG-29 cockpit: the model's own interior (round gauges, HUD frame, canopy arch), with the game's tactical display on a retrofit console in the lower panel.
  • Canopy opens from its rear hinge on the ground and ejects with the seat.
  • Gear doors on the nose and main gear open and close with the gear.
  • Damage display in the cockpit and the map icon are both drawn from the MiG's own outline.
  • Four liveries: Two-Tone Grey, Desert Tan, Digital Grey and Display Blue, available to both factions.
  • Four loading screens rendered from the model.

Install

Requires BepInEx 5 and Blueprinter 2.0.1 or newer.

  1. Install BepInEx 5 into your Nuclear Option folder.
  2. Install Blueprinter into BepInEx/plugins.
  3. Download MiG-29 Fulcrum_x.y.z.nobp from the latest release and put it in BepInEx/plugins/MiG-29_Fulcrum/. Delete any older versions first.
  4. Start the game. The MiG-29 appears in medium hangars, shelters and revetments on land bases, and in the Encyclopedia.

Multiplayer: every player needs the same mod set, and Blueprinter checks this when you join. This is an early release, so please report handling or weapon issues on GitHub Issues, including your controls and your speed and altitude at the time.
