MiG-29 Fulcrum for Nuclear Option - credits and licences
https://github.com/IornMan1213/MiG29-NuclearOption

Install: put "MiG-29 Fulcrum_<version>.nobp" and "MiG29Instruments.dll" in BepInEx/plugins/MiG-29_Fulcrum/.
Requires BepInEx 5 and Blueprinter 2.0.1 or newer.

assets at runtime; no game files are redistributed. Nuclear Option is (c) Shockfront Studios; this is an unofficial fan mod.
